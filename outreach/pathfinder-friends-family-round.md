# Pathfinder Friends & Family Round

## What is Pathfinder?

Pathfinder is a quantum manufacturing company.

We believe the next major advances in quantum computing will come from manufacturing capability rather than new architectures. Today's quantum computers are still built using bespoke equipment with months-long lead times, making progress slower and more expensive than it needs to be.

Our goal is to build the tools and manufacturing capability that make quantum hardware cheaper, faster, and easier to develop.

---

## Why now?

Quantum computing is moving from scientific discovery to engineering.

Governments and industry are investing billions into quantum hardware, yet progress is increasingly constrained by manufacturing: expensive components, fragmented supply chains, and long iteration cycles. As the science matures, the bottleneck is shifting towards the ability to design, build, and test hardware rapidly.

We believe this is the right moment to build the manufacturing layer.

---

## Why I'm building Pathfinder

Over the past year I've immersed myself in quantum hardware, studying the field full-time, developing an integrated trapped-ion system, and building relationships across the UK and Dutch quantum ecosystems.

That process led me to a simple conclusion: the biggest opportunity is not necessarily to invent a fundamentally different quantum computer, but to make it dramatically faster and cheaper to build the ones we already know how to construct.

Pathfinder exists to test that thesis.

---

## What this round will achieve

This funding will allow Pathfinder to transition from a founder-led research effort into an investable deep-tech company.

The immediate objectives are to:

* Recruit a technical cofounder.
* Build the initial engineering and research team.
* Continue developing the company's technical platform.
* Establish relationships with leading researchers, suppliers, and investors.
* Reach the milestones required to raise an institutional pre-seed round.

---

## What the money will be used for

* Founder living costs, enabling me to work on Pathfinder full time.
* Travel to conferences, laboratories, and customer and investor meetings.
* Engineering software, simulation tools, and technical infrastructure.
* Company operating costs, legal expenses, and IP.

## The investment

I'm raising a Friends & Family round to fund the next stage of Pathfinder's development.

This is a high-risk investment in a very early-stage deep-tech company. There is a real possibility investors lose some or all of their investment. I'm only asking people to participate if they're comfortable with that level of risk and believe in both the long-term opportunity and my ability to execute it.

The objective of this round is simple: build the team, validate the thesis, and put Pathfinder in a position to raise institutional capital.
