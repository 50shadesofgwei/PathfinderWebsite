Pathfinder is looking for cofounders and scientific advisors to join the team that will design the production line for quantum computers.

Our thesis is that the standard process of design first and manufacturing second is incorrect. The winning machine is, necessarily, the one that can be built; therefore the winning company is the one that can build the machines.

The people who want to join Pathfinder are the tenacious, the rulebreakers, and the ultra-ambitious. If you’re frustrated that your best ideas are blocked by committees, excessive caution, and the demand for certainty before anything is built, Pathfinder is the place for you. If the phrase “that’s how we’ve always done it” makes your blood boil, Pathfinder is the place for you. If you’re the kind of person that simply has to leave their unique mark upon history, Pathfinder is the place for you.

The scope of work spans experimental atom physics, integrated-photonics chips, and semiconductor manufacturing. Prior work in one of these areas is preferred, but evidence of exceptional ability in any highly technical domain is sufficient.

If successful, the work you do at Pathfinder will revitalise a stagnating field and fight for control of a trillion-dollar industry.
